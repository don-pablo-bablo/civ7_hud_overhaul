The top bar from Pablo's HUD Overhaul on its own: more pills, and a compact and a detailed tooltip on each. It adds them to whatever top bar you have, the game's own or another mod's, and leaves the rest of the screen alone.

Want the whole screen reworked, with a theme, a World Tracker and a compact leader ribbon? [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3819347599]Pablo's HUD Overhaul[/url] has all of this and more.

[h2]More pills[/h2]

It adds these to the game's gold, science, culture, happiness, influence and settlements, each in its own colour:

[list]
[*]Celebrations, in the happiness pill
[*]Food
[*]Production
[*]Cities and towns, with your population, in the settlements pill
[*]Units, military and civilian
[*]Happiness levels of your settlements
[*]Trade routes
[*]Resources
[*]Treasure convoys
[*]Great works
[*]Religion
[*]Victory standings
[/list]

The pills sit on two rows, or on one row if you choose. On a small screen, with large text or with a bigger top bar, any that do not fit go on another row; turn off the ones you need least to keep them on fewer. Religion and treasure convoys show only when they apply.

[h2]Tooltips[/h2]

Hover a pill for its compact tooltip: where a yield comes from, settlement by settlement, with the treasury, current research or next celebration. Each settlement has an icon for a capital, city or town.

Click the pill, or press the tooltip inspect key, K by default, for its detailed tooltip, and again to close it. It breaks each settlement's yield down by source, specialists and upkeep included, with a bar for its share, red for a loss. In a detailed tooltip, click:

[list]
[*][b]the title and values[/b] to open the screen the pill used to open, such as the yields report;
[*][b]a settlement, or any line under it,[/b] to move the map there, or for a trade route, to the settlement it trades with;
[*][b]a victory's leaders[/b] to open its tab on the victories screen;
[*][b]a unit[/b] to select it and move the camera to it;
[*][b]a group's heading[/b], such as Cities or a victory, to close or open it.
[/list]

While a detailed tooltip is open, the other pills show no tooltip on hover, so nothing pops up behind it. Click another pill to open its detailed tooltip instead.

[h2]Options[/h2]

In Options > Add-ons: the top bar's size and the tooltips' size, from 75% to 150%; one or two rows; and each added pill on or off.

[h2]With Pablo's HUD Overhaul[/h2]

This mod does nothing while Pablo's HUD Overhaul is on, which has all of it. Each keeps its own settings.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

It has been played with all of these enabled at once:

[list]
[*][b]Map Trix[/b]: its option for coloured top bar entries gives way to these pills.
[*][b]No Gradients[/b]: it still removes the screen's vignette, and the pills sit on its top bar.
[*][b]Readable Happiness Icons[/b]: its icons for each happiness level show in the happiness levels pill and its tooltip.
[*][b]Drongo's Leader Ribbons[/b]: the pills go on another row rather than run under its ribbon, as they do with the game's own.
[*][b]Pablo's Better Yield Tooltip[/b]: its tile tooltips work alongside these.
[/list]

[h2]Saves[/h2]

The mod only changes the interface. You can add it to a game in progress or remove it without breaking the save.
